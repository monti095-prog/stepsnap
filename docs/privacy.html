StepSnap privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What StepSnap does with your data

StepSnap only works while you press Start recording. During a recording it reads, on the pages you visit: the text and role of elements you click, text you type (except in password and payment-type fields, which are never recorded), the page address and title, and a screenshot of the visible tab for each step.

Where it goes

All of this is stored locally in your browser using the extension storage. It is used only to build your guide and PDF on your computer. StepSnap has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. It does not send, sell, or share any data with anyone.

Your control

You can pause or stop at any time. Use Discard on the guide page to delete the steps and screenshots, or remove the extension to delete all of its data. Review your guide before sharing it, because typed text and screenshots may contain personal information.
