Do the task once. Get the guide.

StepSnap records your clicks and typing, adds a screenshot to every step, and turns it all into a guide and a PDF. No account. Nothing leaves your computer.

Download for Chrome, Edge, Brave View source on GitHub

Free and open source. Install takes about a minute.

Install in five steps

  1. Download the zip above and extract it. Keep the extracted folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge, edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder, the one with manifest.json inside.
  5. Pin StepSnap from the puzzle-piece menu, click it, and press Start recording.

What you get

  • A numbered step for every click, typed entry, dropdown choice, and Enter press.
  • A screenshot per step with a red box around the element you used.
  • An editable guide: rename, reorder, or delete steps.
  • A PDF that downloads automatically when you stop recording.

Your data stays with you

StepSnap has no server and no sign-in. Steps and screenshots are kept in your browser until you discard them, and password fields are never recorded. Read the privacy policy.
